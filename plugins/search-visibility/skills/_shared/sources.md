## Terms we use

| Term | Meaning here |
| --- | --- |
| **SEO** | Eligible, crawlable, people-first pages that can rank in classic results |
| **AEO** | Extractable direct answers (featured snippet, voice, one-slot answers) |
| **AIO** | Visibility in Google AI Overviews / AI Mode (Google's "AI optimization") |
| **GEO** | Being retrieved, cited, or recommended inside a generated answer, including non-Google engines |

AIO is not "use AI to write SEO pages." That is a content-ops label we do
not use.
