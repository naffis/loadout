# Plain-English brief (search-visibility)

Copy the core-engineering brief: the reader wants what is true, what to
do, and any decision. Do not dump the checklist.

**What's going on.** The page or site, in product words. Indexed or not,
thin or unique, blocked or crawlable.

**What we need to do.** The next move (rewrite, metadata, robots, leave
it). No citation promises.

**Decision.** Only if they must choose (allow a crawler, publish vs
kill a commodity page).

Then the next-prompt fence when the skill requires one.
